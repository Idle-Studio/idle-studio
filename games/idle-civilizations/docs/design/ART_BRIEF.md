## Style Guide

- **Overall tone**: Epic, timeless, slightly stylised (not photorealistic)
- **Colour palette**: Each era should feel distinct — match the `levelColors` in the JSON
- **Stone Age**: Warm ochres, earth tones (#8B7355 primary)
- **Bronze Age**: Copper and terracotta (#CD7F32 primary)
- **Ancient Empires**: Gold and sand (#C4A44D primary)
- **Classical World**: Marble blue and white (#5B8DB8 primary)
- **Medieval Era**: Deep purple and stone (#6B4F7C primary)
- **Renaissance**: Burnt orange and sienna (#C44B24 primary)
- **Industrial**: Steel grey and smoke (#607080 primary)
- **Space Age**: Deep navy and electric blue (#1E4A7E primary)
- **UI overlay**: All assets must work over the dark background (#0D0D0F)
