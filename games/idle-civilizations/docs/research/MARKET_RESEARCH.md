## Competitive Analysis

### Direct Competitors (idle + civilization theme)

No dominant player owns the civilization/history niche in idle games. The closest are:

| Game | Overlap | Gap We Fill |
|------|---------|-------------|
| Idle Civilization (various clones) | Name only | No real historical accuracy, no wonder system |
| Forge of Empires | Civilization theme | Turn-based strategy, not idle |
| Rise of Kingdoms | Real-time strategy | Heavy PvP, not idle |
| AdVenture Capitalist | Business empire | No historical narrative |

**Opportunity:** The civilization theme is the #1 most recognizable idle game concept that has no market leader.

### Successful Adjacent Games (learn from these)

**Egg, Inc.** — Lessons:
- Prestige system creates infinite replayability
- Shareable "coop" cards drove massive organic social spread
- Offline income is the primary retention mechanic
- Simple art, strong number satisfaction

**Gold & Goblins** — Lessons:
- Strong visual identity (goblins, mine depth) = instantly recognizable creatives
- Clear progression depth (deeper mines) = reason to return
- Rewarded ads framed as helping (extra gold) not interrupting

**Carnival Tycoon** — Lessons:
- Environmental feedback (rides build, visuals update) = high session satisfaction
- 4+ sessions per day average driven by "what did my park build while I was away?"
- IAP-driven more than ads (38% ads / 62% IAP) — cosmetic bundles work

## Target Audience

### Primary Segment: Casual Gamers (25–44)
- Play in short bursts (commute, lunch, evening)
- Value progress that continues while they work
- Appreciate educational/historical content
- Respond to collecting and completing sets
- Not price-sensitive to €1.99–€4.99 if value is clear

### Secondary Segment: Idle Game Veterans (18–35)
- Know the genre, actively seek new themes
- Will engage deeply with prestige mechanics
- Likely to buy Civ Pass for subscription value
- Important for leaderboard seeding and word-of-mouth

### Tertiary Segment: History Enthusiasts (all ages)
- Attracted by historical accuracy and era flavor text
- More likely to share milestones ("I just built the Colosseum!")
- Strong App Store review writers (important for ASO)

## User Acquisition Strategy

### Channels (priority order)
1. **TikTok Ads** — satisfying idle number-go-up content, era transition videos
2. **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** — lookalike audiences from soft launch
3. **Apple Search Ads** — high-intent iOS users searching "idle game", "civilization game"
4. **App Store featuring** — pitch to Apple editorial with education/history angle
5. **Organic/ASO** — optimize title, screenshots, ratings

### Creative Concepts
- "I started with a campfire. Now I have the Eiffel Tower." (time-lapse)
- Era transition moment: primitive → modern (emotional)
- "Your civilization earns while you sleep" (offline income visualization)
- Leaderboard climb: "From #5,000 to #12 in one week"
