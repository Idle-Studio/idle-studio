## Core Concept

Players build a football club from a ragtag Sunday League side to Champions League champions. Each division represents a progression level, with iconic stadiums as milestones and legendary players as characters.

**Core fantasy:** "I'm building the next Manchester City, one idle match at a time."

## Planned Milestones (Trophies / Stadiums)

Each division earns a Trophy or Stadium milestone:
- Sunday League Cup (Division 1)
- County Trophy (Division 2)
- National Stadium (Division 3)
- League Cup (Division 4)
- The Premier League Trophy (Division 5)
- Europa League Trophy (Division 6)
- Champions League Trophy (Division 7)
- Ballon d'Or / World Club Cup (Division 8)

## Seasonal Events

Tied to the real football calendar:
- **Transfer Window** (January/July): Player transfer multiplier event
- **El Clásico Week**: Rivalry matchup event with double production
- **World Cup Year**: Special global tournament event
- **End of Season**: Final week sprint with leaderboard prizes

---

## Unique Considerations

- Tied to a real calendar (football season) — weekly events must align with match days
- Strong European appeal (massive market in Romania and across EU)
- Potential partnership angle: if budget allows, license some real club names
- Without license: use fictional club names with clear real-world inspirations
- Cross-promotion from Game #1 (Civilizations) players: historical empires → football empires
