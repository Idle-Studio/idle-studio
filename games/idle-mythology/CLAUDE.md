## Core Concept

Players build a pantheon of gods across the world's great mythologies — Greek, Norse, Egyptian, Hindu, Aztec, Japanese, and beyond. Each mythology is a level, gods and temples are units, legendary artifacts are milestones.

**Core fantasy:** "I'm running Olympus as an idle empire. Zeus would approve."

## Unique Considerations

- Broad appeal: mythology is universal and educational
- Sensitive content: handle all mythologies with equal respect (no hierarchy of "real" vs "lesser" religions)
- Strong visual identity: each pantheon has distinct art style (Greek marble, Egyptian gold, Norse stone)
- Characters: famous mythological figures (Zeus, Odin, Ra, Shiva, Quetzalcoatl, Amaterasu)
