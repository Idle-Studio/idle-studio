## Core Concept

Players build a restaurant empire from a humble street food cart to a global fine-dining empire with multiple Michelin stars. Each cuisine era represents a different culinary tradition from world history.

**Core fantasy:** "I'm Gordon Ramsay, but idle and with better numbers."

## Planned Milestones (Michelin Stars)

Each cuisine era has 1–2 Michelin Stars to earn. Stars are the Wonders of this game — they:
- Require Gold + secondary resource (e.g., Foot Traffic, Wine)
- Take 30 min – 8 hours to "earn" (restaurant inspection / certification)
- Give a permanent era-scope production bonus
- Remain visually on the restaurant map after prestige

## Tone

Warm and enthusiastic about food. Light puns welcome. References real culinary history without being a food documentary. Should feel like a love letter to food culture.

Example flavor text (draft):
- Street Food Cart: "Every great empire started with a cart. Yours smells amazing."
- Sushi Bar: "Fish freshness is everything. Your suppliers are very, very fast."
- Michelin Fine Dining: "Three stars. You've arrived. Please don't look at the bill."
