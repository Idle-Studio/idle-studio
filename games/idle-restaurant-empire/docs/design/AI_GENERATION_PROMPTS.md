# Idle Restaurant Empire — AI Generation Prompts

Use these prompts with **Midjourney**, **DALL-E 3**, **Stable Diffusion**, or **Adobe Firefly** for images.
Use **Suno** or **Udio** for ambient audio tracks.

---

## Global Style Reference

Paste this at the start of any image prompt to anchor the style:

> Warm vibrant food editorial illustration style, semi-flat design, not cartoon, not hyper-realistic. Deep burgundy backgrounds, amber and gold highlights, cream text areas. Clean and readable at small sizes, rich in detail at full size. Mobile idle game art quality.
