## Core Concept

Players discover humanity's greatest inventions and scientific breakthroughs — from fire and the wheel to quantum computing and fusion energy. Each research period represents a scientific age.

**Core fantasy:** "I'm a genius inventor with unlimited funding and an idle game."

## Milestones (Nobel Prizes / Breakthroughs)

Each period earns 1–2 major "Breakthrough" milestones — equivalent to Wonders. Named after real discoveries:
- Wheel (Ancient)
- Heliocentric Model (Renaissance)
- Theory of Relativity (Modern Physics)
- Moon Landing (Space Science)
- World Wide Web (Digital Age)

---

## Educational Angle

This game has the strongest educational angle of the portfolio. Flavor text can include real scientific facts. This is a strong pitch for App Store featuring (educational category).

Example: "Theory of Relativity — E=mc². Einstein proved mass and energy are equivalent. Your production is now equivalently massive."
